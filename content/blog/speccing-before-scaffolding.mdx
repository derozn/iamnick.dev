Before this blog had routes it had paperwork: a discovery document with a numbered
decision log, product requirements, four architecture decision records and a designed
delivery workflow. That order was the point.

The spec pass caught the expensive mistake while it was still free. The original plan
had the blog inheriting the site's carnival styling by default — an assumption nobody
had questioned because nobody had written it down. Seeing it in an ADR made it
visible, and it survived less than an hour: the carnival is an experience, not the
brand, and the blog needed its own identity.

A decision that reverses in an afternoon of document review is a bargain. The same
reversal after two stages of build would have been a rewrite.
