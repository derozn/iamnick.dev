Before this blog had routes it had a decision log. Nineteen rows of it. That order was
deliberate, and it paid off inside a day.

The original plan had the blog inheriting the site's carnival styling. Nobody decided
that. It was just the default, because nobody had written it down and looked at it.
Seeing it in an ADR made it obvious. I'd made an error: the carnival is an experience
on my site, not my brand. That ADR was accepted and reversed within the hour, and the
riskiest stage of the whole build plan got deleted with it.

An afternoon of arguing with documents versus a rewrite two stages in. I'll take the
paperwork.
